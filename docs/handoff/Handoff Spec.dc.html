08

Scroll choreography

Progress is measured against the seven beat sections, never raw document height, so header and footer chrome cannot shift a beat away from its panel. The viewport centre selects the active beat, u is the centre's normalised position through that section, and p is (beatIndex + u) divided by 7. Beat k therefore occupies the k-th seventh of p at every width, in the 100vh desktop beats and the auto-height stacked beats alike. Phases split at u 0.25 and 0.75.
